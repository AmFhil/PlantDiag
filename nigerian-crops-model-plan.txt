1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & THE PLANTVILLAGE FLAW
================================================================================
The current AgriNovva deployment uses a 44.8 MB Float32 ResNet-18 model trained
on the 38-class academic PlantVillage dataset. While effective as an initial
proof-of-concept, it suffers from two fatal flaws when deployed in Nigeria:

1. TEMPERATE BIAS (~42% Irrelevant Classes):
   16 of the 38 classes (Apple, Blueberry, Cherry, Peach, Raspberry, Strawberry,
   Grape) are temperate crops that do not grow in Nigeria's agro-ecological zones.
   Meanwhile, Nigeria's primary food staples (Cassava, Yam, Cowpea, Rice,
   Sorghum, Millet) are completely absent.

2. LAB-TO-FIELD DOMAIN SHIFT:
   PlantVillage was photographed in indoor laboratory conditions against uniform
   gray/black paper backgrounds with controlled studio lighting.
   When tested on real Nigerian smallholder farms (harsh tropical sunlight, dusty
   leaves, farmers holding leaves, background soil, shadows, and camera blur on
   budget Tecno/Itel phones), accuracy plummets from 95%+ in lab tests to <40%
   in field conditions.

OBJECTIVE:
Replace the temperate PlantVillage model with a dedicated "NaijaStaples-30"
model covering over 85% of Nigerian agricultural production, trained on real
in-situ field imagery, and quantized to INT8 (<10 MB) for instant offline
inference on 2GB RAM Android phones.

2. NIGERIAN STAPLE CROPS & PRIORITY VALUE CHAINS
================================================================================
Nigeria's agricultural security rests on four primary crop clusters:

1. ROOT & TUBER STAPLES (Southern & Middle Belt):
   - Cassava: Nigeria produces ~60M metric tonnes/yr (world's #1). Major diseases:
     * Cassava Mosaic Disease (CMD) - transmitted by whitefly Bemisia tabaci
     * Cassava Brown Streak Disease (CBSD) - destroys edible root tissue
     * Cassava Bacterial Blight (CBB) - Xanthomonas axonopodis
     * Cassava Green Mite (CGM) - causes leaf distortion and chlorosis
   - Yam: Nigeria produces ~70% of global yam supply (~50M tonnes). Diseases:
     * Yam Anthracnose (Colletotrichum gloeosporioides) - black leaf scorch
     * Yam Mosaic Virus (YMV) - leaf mottle and stunting

2. GRAIN LEGUMES & CEREALS (Northern & Middle Belt):
   - Cowpea (Beans): Nigeria is the world's largest producer & consumer. Diseases:
     * Cercospora Leaf Spot (Cercospora canescens)
     * Cowpea Bacterial Blight (Xanthomonas phaseoli)
     * Maruca Pod Borer damage
   - Maize (Corn): Critical national staple. Diseases & Pests:
     * Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda) - devastating whorl damage
     * Northern Corn Leaf Blight (Exserohilum turcicum)
     * Common Rust (Puccinia sorghi)
   - Rice: National consumer staple. Diseases:
     * Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae)
     * Bacterial Leaf Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae)
     * Brown Spot (Bipolaris oryzae)
   - Sorghum (Guinea Corn): Northern arid zone staple. Diseases:
     * Sorghum Anthracnose (Colletotrichum sublineolum)
     * Striga hermonthica (parasitic weed infestation symptoms)

3. HORTICULTURAL VALUE CHAINS (Nationwide):
   - Tomato:
     * Early Blight (Alternaria solani)
     * Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
     * Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus (TYLCV)
     * Tuta absoluta (leaf miner damage)
     * Bacterial Spot (Xanthomonas)
   - Pepper (Hot & Sweet):
     * Bacterial Spot
     * Pepper Leaf Curl Virus

5. MODEL ARCHITECTURE & MOBILE OPTIMIZATION
================================================================================
COMPARISON:

Feature              | Current ResNet-18 (Float32) | Target MobileNetV3-Large (INT8)
---------------------|-----------------------------|--------------------------------
Model File Size      | 44.8 MB                     | ~9.5 MB - 11.2 MB (-78%)
Inference Latency    | 250ms - 480ms               | 45ms - 85ms on Helio G25/G35
Memory Footprint     | 50 MB mmap / heap spikes    | 12 MB zero-copy direct buffer
Accuracy on Field    | <40% (lab domain shift)     | 88% - 93% on field validation
Quantization Scheme  | None (Float32)              | Full Integer INT8 (Weights & Acts)
AAPT Compression     | noCompress 'tflite'         | noCompress 'tflite' (mmap enabled)

KEY ARCHITECTURAL ADVANTAGES:
1. MobileNetV3 uses Hard-Swish and depthwise separable convolutions tuned for
   ARM NEON SIMD instructions present in low-cost MediaTek and Unisoc processors.
2. Full post-training INT8 quantization replaces 32-bit floating-point math with
   8-bit integer matrix multiplications, executing 4x faster on mobile CPUs.
3. Zero-loss knowledge distillation: The model is initialized from ImageNet-1K
   weights and fine-tuned using cosine annealing learning rates.

6. FIELD DATA AUGMENTATION PIPELINE (ALBUMENTATIONS)
================================================================================
To eliminate the lab-to-field domain shift, training uses heavy data augmentation
mimicking Nigerian farm conditions:

1. Tropical Sun Simulation:
   - RandomBrightnessContrast (brightness_limit=0.25, contrast_limit=0.25)
   - ColorJitter (saturation=0.3, hue=0.05) to simulate dusty/wilted leaves
2. Motion & Hardware Noise:
   - MotionBlur and GaussianBlur to simulate trembling hands on cheap camera sensors
   - GaussNoise for low-light morning/dusk capture
3. Physical Leaf Occlusions:
   - CoarseDropout (cutout) to simulate hands holding the leaf, holes from insects,
     and dirt specks.
4. Scale & Perspective:
   - RandomResizedCrop (scale 0.7 to 1.0) to simulate variable distance from leaf.
